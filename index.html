How It Works

Three simple steps to analyze any content for threats.

Step 01 📋
Paste Content
Copy any email, SMS, link, or message and paste it into the scanner box above.
Step 02 🤖
AI Analysis
Claude AI examines the content for phishing patterns, suspicious links, urgency tactics, and other threat signals.
Step 03 📊
Get Verdict
Receive a detailed verdict with confidence score, key signals, and explanation of the reasoning.

Verdict Levels

Content is classified into one of three threat levels.

✅
Safe
Content appears legitimate with no notable threat signals detected. Proceed normally.
⚠️
Suspicious
Content has some red flags worth caution. Verify the sender and avoid clicking unknown links.
🚨
Dangerous
High-confidence threat detected. Do not click links, share personal info, or respond.
